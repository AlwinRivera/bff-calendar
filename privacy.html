Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

bff calendar helps friends see when everyone is busy or free, across time zones. We collect only what that needs, and we don't sell it or show ads.

What we store

  • Your profile: nickname, emoji, color, time zone, usual sleep hours and an optional status.
  • Your plans: busy and "free to call" times. Labels are saved only if you turn off "keep it private".
  • Imported calendar files: we read the file on your device and save only the start and end times for the next 5 weeks. Event names are saved only if you choose "show event names".
  • Your circles: which circles you're in and their invite codes.
  • Your email, only if you add one to keep your account.

Who can see it

Only people in a circle you've joined can see your profile and plans. People outside your circles can't see anything. Anyone with a circle's invite code can join it, so only share codes with people you trust.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the data and handles sign-in (servers in Singapore).
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code, so they see your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

Plans older than about a week are deleted automatically. Everything else stays until you delete your account.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile and delete plans anytime in the Me tab. Delete my account (also in the Me tab) permanently removes your profile, plans and memberships. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 you can also ask to access or correct your data, object to its use, or file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Age

bff calendar is meant for people 13 and older.

Contact

Questions or requests: reach the app owner, Alwin Rivera, at github.com/AlwinRivera.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
